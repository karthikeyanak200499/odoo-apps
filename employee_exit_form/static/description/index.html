Employee Exit Form (Dept → HR → CAO)

Odoo 17 Community & Enterprise Category: Human Resources / Employees License: LGPL-3 or OPL-1

Employee Exit Form streamlines exit interviews, approvals, and clearances with a guided workflow from Department Head to HR to CAO, plus signatures, emails, and asset returns.

Features

  • Exit interview form with reasons, feedback, and instructions
  • Approval flow: Dept Head → HR → CAO (buttons & statusbar)
  • Signatures & dates (employee, HR, CAO)
  • One2many: Returned equipment (description, serial, date)
  • Smart buttons (archive/open employee, proceed to HR)
  • Mail templates (submit, HR, CAO approved/rejected)
  • Record rules per role & multi-company support
  • Auto defaults (interview date, employee)
  • Computed acknowledgements with bold name & code
  • Clean uninstall; minimal dependencies

Workflow

  1. Employee submits → Department Manager reviews & proceeds to HR
  2. HR validates, assigns CAO, and forwards
  3. CAO approves/rejects → notifications sent
  4. On completion: archive employee/user, close contracts, finalize
