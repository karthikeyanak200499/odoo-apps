Employee Exit Form

Odoo 17 Community & Enterprise Human Resources / Employees License: LGPL-3 or OPL-1

Overview

Employee Exit Form streamlines exit interviews, approvals, asset returns, and notifications with a clean, guided flow.

Key Features

✅ Approvals

Statusbar: Dept Head → HR → CAO → HR.

🖋 Signatures

Binary image signatures with dates.

📦 Asset Returns

One2many table for items and serials.

✉️ Emails

Submit, HR, CAO approve/reject templates.

🔒 Rules

Role-based record rules; multi-company.

Workflow

1
User submits → Dept Head reviews & proceeds to HR
2
HR validates, assigns CAO
3
CAO approves/rejects → notifications sent
4
HR archives employee/user, closes contracts
