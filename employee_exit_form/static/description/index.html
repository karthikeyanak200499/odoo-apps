Employee Exit Form Workflow

Odoo 17 Community & Enterprise Human Resources License: LGPL-3 / OPL-1

Employee Exit Form automates the entire offboarding process with approvals, notifications, and asset tracking — ensuring smooth clearance from Department Head to HR to CAO.

Key Features

Exit interview form with reasons, feedback, and final notes
Approval flow: Dept Head → HR → CAO with status tracking
Digital signatures & auto-filled dates for all stakeholders
Track returned assets with description, serial, and date
Smart buttons to archive/open employee & proceed to HR
Email templates for submission, approval, and rejection
Multi-company & role-based access control
Automatic defaults for interview date and employee name

Workflow

Employee submits exit form → Department Manager reviews
HR validates details, assigns CAO, and forwards
CAO approves/rejects → automatic notifications
On completion: Archive employee, close contracts, finalize
